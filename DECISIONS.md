# Decisions

Choices made where the spec was silent or ambiguous (spec instruction: pick the simplest option and record it here). Numbers referenced live in `src/data/balance.json` unless noted.

## A shorter wait for the first Legendary (Nov 11, newest)

Kevin: "we're still waiting so long for the first legendary to appear".

Timed on the built page with a quick player: the Legendary landed 19 s after PLAY, and 13 s of that was watching three warm-up spins at the normal pace (3.5 s each) before the tutorial's own spin. The taps before it (World 1, START THE FIGHT!, Roll, the lock) take about 6 s and are the player's own.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Two warm-up spins instead of three (Junk, then Shiny; `tutorial.warmupRarities`), and quick ones: 1.3 s of spinning and 0.7 s on the find (`warmupSpinSeconds`, `warmupRestSeconds`) against the usual 2.6 s and 0.9 s. The tutorial's own spin keeps its full 2.6 s. The Legendary now lands 6.6 s after the lock comes off (13.1 s before), about 12.7 s after PLAY for a quick player (19.2 s). Replaces E5's three spins and E12's normal pace. | Still "a bit" of rolling first, as Kevin asked in E5, at half the wait. The build-up before the Legendary is what the wait is for, so it stays whole. Each find stays listed under the reel while the next spins, so it can still be read. |
| W2 | Each warm-up find is for a gear slot of its own, and never the tutorial item's (`warmupSlot` in roller.ts). | With only two warm-up finds picked at random, the first Equip best put on fewer than three items in 29% of new games (86 of 300), and the tutorial had to ask for Equip best again. Now it always puts on three: the cone and both finds. It also ends T10's case for good: a warm-up find can no longer be a plain traffic cone. |
| W3 | Beginner's luck stays where it was: it starts about 35 s after the lock comes off and lands about 45 s in (300 simulated new games: median 35.6 s, 44.6 s). | Kevin asked for that timing earlier; only the first find moved. |

Before: the bot beat World 20 at about 5 h 16 min, rebirthed four more times with longer and longer gaps (11 min, 1 h 17, 1 h 55, 5 h 13), then nothing happened from hour 14 to hour 24. Every ladder had a top (20 worlds, all items by Rebirth 5, level 100, Grail), and from Rebirth 9 every rebirth asked for World 20 and 4 times the gold for about 8% more luck.

## Luck token combos: tokens landing together multiply (Nov 5)

Kevin: "if two numbers both land on a multiplier, create an animation for it and make the multiplier those two numbers combined / if 2x and 2x, both of them would be 4x / if 2x and 5x / both the numbers would be 10x / if its three numbers at the same time, same thing, multiply".

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Two or more reels landing a luck token in the same round (the same step of their chains) make a combo: each of those reels counts the product of that round's tokens. ×2 and ×2 make ×4 on both, ×2 and ×5 make ×10 on both, ×2, ×3 and ×5 make ×30 on all three. A reel's own chain multiplies on top: ×3 then ×2 on one reel, with a ×5 beside its ×3, is ×30 on it and ×15 on the other. A reel with no token that round isn't in it. This replaces M1: the same token on every reel now multiplies by itself once per reel (×5 on three reels is ×125 each; it was ×25), and different tokens count too. The combo's luck counts for the chain floors, so ×2, ×3 and ×5 together guarantee an Epic or better on all three. Offline rolls follow the same rule. (`comboSteps`, `comboValue`, `tokenMult` in roller.ts. The spin's `matched` keeps its name, now the combo rounds, so saves load as before; a spin saved mid-roll keeps the rounds it was rolled with.) | Kevin's rule, word for word. |
| C2 | How often (base token chance): one spin in 400 with two reels, 140 with three, 70 with four and 45 with five. The old match came about once in 900 spins on two reels. The rolls are made exactly as before (only the sum on them changed), so seeded runs play out the same until a combo comes up. | |
| C3 | On the Roll tab a combo plays as its last token lands. Each reel's number pops out of its reel and flies to the middle. As they meet, two diamond rings and sparks burst, the machine jolts and flashes, and their product slams in big under "COMBO!", with the sum that made it ("×2 × ×5") under it. It beats a few times, then copies of it drop onto each of those reels' chips, which then show the new total in gold, without counting up to it. Sounds: a zip as the numbers fly, then a thump and a bell per reel as they merge, under the jingle. Reduce motion: nothing flies; the sum shows over the reels for 1.4 s and the chips change at once. Reduce flashing: no rings, flash or bright slam. | Kevin: "create an animation for it". |
| C4 | The product holds 0.8 s, or less when the next reel stops sooner (never under 0.4 s), so it doesn't sit over a landing. Everything in it is timed in CSS from the moment it mounts, so the burst lands exactly as the numbers meet even when the page is busy (a timer left a number waiting in the middle for 0.15 s); the jolt and flash are delayed on the same clock. It blinks out by visibility instead of fading: Chrome drew a fading layer's middle frame again as the combo was taken away. | Found in frame-by-frame recordings: 2-reel ×2 + ×2, 3-reel ×2 + ×5 and ×2 + ×3 + ×5, Reduce motion. |
| C5 | The battle's luck rows: each row in the combo flashes its token gold and its total jumps to the combo's in gold, and "COMBO! ×10" pops up under the rows with the sum under it while the battle kicks. | The same moment, on any tab. |
| C6 | Admin portal: "Next: combo ×2 × ×5" (two reels) and "×2 × ×3 × ×5" (three), through a new per-reel token cheat. The every-reel button now says what it gives (×250 to the power of the reels). | To see a combo on demand. |

## Saves that don't reset, a luck stack you can't miss, an admin portal, a bug sweep (Nov 4)

Kevin: "u gotta stop restarting my game save every time"; "play tester said they didnt notice the tiers stack, you need to make it more noticable, esp when it gets to a higher number"; "Everytime I try to upgrade my luck it just crashes"; "i need u to go and fix all bugs and crashes PLEASE just do a complete full sweep"; "give me an admin portal where can give myself money and gems and stuff".

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | The game is also saved in the player's Claude account when it runs as a claude.ai artifact. It is a private document in the artifact's `db` store (`data/users/<id>/save`; the page declares `db` and `user`). It is written about once a minute while playing and whenever the game goes to the background. On start, the newer of the browser's save and the account's (by play time) is played, and "Your save came back from your Claude account!" says so. The loading screen gives the account up to 2.5 s to answer. If it answers later, it is still taken while the title screen is up, or the game restarts into it if it has more play in it. Start a new game clears the account's copy too. | The viewer's own browser storage can come back empty (the platform says so: cleared or partitioned storage, the phone app's web view, another device), and then the game started over. Only the artifact's owner and colleagues at Contributor or above can write there; anyone else keeps the browser save alone, as before. |
| S2 | A save that doesn't pass the checks is mended, not thrown away. Each bad value goes back to a new game's, a broken entry in a list (a bag item, say) is dropped, and after a few rounds a section still failing is reset whole. The player is told how many values were reset. Numbers are never written as something the next load rejects: NaN and Infinity used to be written as null, which failed the whole save. Luck and the record find score can no longer become NaN or Infinity. | Before, one bad value anywhere made `deserialize` return null and the game start fresh, and the next three autosaves pushed the real save out of the backups. |
| S3 | A main save that can't be loaded at all is copied to a rescue slot before anything can overwrite it, and Settings → Your save offers to load it. | So it can't be lost the way it used to be. |
| S4 | Settings → Your save also says where the game is kept, gives a save code (the whole game, gzipped, about 6 KB: "DL1:...") to copy, and loads a pasted code after a second tap. | A backup the player keeps, and a way to move a game between devices or browsers, whatever the storage does. |
| L1 | The luck stack is shown where the eyes are, on the reels. The badge on a reel counts up to each new total (×2, ×4, ×7, ×10), and grows and changes color as it climbs: green, blue from ×10, purple from ×25 (an Epic or better guaranteed), gold from ×50 (Legendary or better), cycling rainbow from ×250 (Mythic or better). A total never runs past the view's edge: in the huge face it drops the word LUCK. | Kevin: "esp when it gets to a higher number". |

Playtesters don't see it unless they find the taps. |

| B2 | Buying Luck: the crash didn't happen here. Every Luck tier was bought one by one and by holding Buy, on fresh and late saves, upright and sideways, with no errors. The Bag, Worlds and an item's card redraw when what they show changes; they redrew ten times a second with every battle tick, 180 to 200 ms of script a second on a PC and 3 to 5 times that on a phone. | # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|

## Redesign (Nov 2): always-rolling RNG loop

Kevin asked for a Roblox-style rolling game: the dumpster rolls on its own, items carry a power number, a limited bag keeps the best ones, extra luck sources, an upgrade grid, rebirths that raise luck, and enchantments. These rows replace the older ones marked *superseded* below.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | *Superseded by V1 and V2.* No dive button. A free roll happens every 2.0 s (the Roll Speed upgrade brings it down to 0.4 s), and Trash Bags won in fights are spent automatically as bonus rolls, 3 per second. | "Constantly rolling without having to click." |
| R2 | *Odds updated by W9.* Rarities are "1 in X" chances: Decent 3, Shiny 10, Epic 50, Legendary 400, Mythic 5,000, Mint in Box 100,000. A roll tries the rarest tier first, then each lower tier, and each try succeeds with chance min(1, luck / X). The Luck popup lists the exact per-roll chances, computed by the same function the roller uses. | The RNG-game convention. The odds shown are always the real ones (playbook). |
| R3 | Luck = (1 + Luck upgrades + 0.05 per Dumpster Level above 1 + Lucky enchantments) x rebirth multiplier x Luck Potion. Bad-luck protection is unchanged: from Dumpster Level 3, after 40 rolls with nothing Epic or better, the next roll is guaranteed Epic or better. | Dumpster upgrades still help, plus several new luck sources. |
| R4 | Every 10th roll is a Lucky Roll at x2 luck, shown on a meter. The Lucky Rolls upgrade lowers 10 to as few as 4. | A visible "something good is coming" beat between rare pulls. |
| R5 | *Auto-equip removed by V3; the bag rules still apply.* Items have a power number. A roll is equipped automatically when it beats the equipped item's power (Auto-equip can be switched off in the Bag); otherwise it goes in the bag. The bag holds 20 items (+2 per Bag Space level), sorted by power. When it is full, the weakest unlocked item, or the new roll if it is weaker still, is scrapped for gold and XP. Locked items are never scrapped automatically. | "Keeps the highest-rated items." |
| R6 | Scrapping pays half the old recycle coins (times gold multipliers) and a quarter of the XP. Enchanted items pay +50% per enchantment tier. | Constant rolling would otherwise flood the economy. |
| R7 | *Superseded by V6.* The upgrade grid costs gold: Luck, Gold Gain, Roll Speed, Bag Space, Lucky Rolls, Enchanting, Strength (ATK%), Toughness (HP%), Bag Finder. Cost = base x growth^level, with a level cap per upgrade. Holding the buy button keeps buying. Upgrades reset on rebirth. | |
| R8 | *Superseded by W8 and V8.* Rebirth needs stage 10 cleared (+5 stages per rebirth, capped at the last stage) and 5,000 gold in hand (x4 per rebirth). Each rebirth gives luck x(1 + 0.25 per rebirth) and gold x(1 + 0.2 per rebirth), and resets stage progress, gold and upgrades. Items, the bag, player level, Dumpster Level, the Codex and Gems are kept. It takes two taps to confirm. | Keeping the bag and level makes rebirths feel like a boost instead of a loss. |
| R9 | Enchantments: each roll has a 5% chance (+1.5% per Enchanting level) of 1 to 3 enchantments (80/17/3%), each of tier I, II or III (70/25/5%). Every tier adds 15/30/55% item power. Lucky and Golden add luck and gold; Swift, Vampiric, Giant and Magnetic add attack speed, lifesteal, HP and bag find; Flaming burns, Thundering zaps the next enemy, Echoing strikes twice, Explosive makes kills splash, Thorny reflects damage, and Prismatic raises the item's rarity by 1 or 2 tiers. All of it is in `enchants.json`. | "Add power level and rarity, but also do interesting things." |
| R10 | Luck Potions give x2 luck for 60 s. One drops from every mini-boss and boss kill, and there is a 0.5% chance per regular wave. A new potion adds its time to one that is still running. | One more luck source, tied to fights. |
| R11 | Enemies got x2.4 HP and x1.7 ATK. | With a constant stream of gear, fights were over too fast to read and stages fell too quickly (simulator). |
| R12 | Save version 2. The migration moves an undecided dive item into the bag, adds the enchantment and lock fields, and drops Auto-Dive. | |
| R13 | *Timing updated by V2.* Legendary and better rolls get a short build-up (the dumpster shakes and glows in the rarity's color, then a silence drop) and a full-screen reveal. Big rolls that land during a reveal wait their turn, up to 3. Epic gets a sting and a burst; lower rarities get a pop from the dumpster. | Big moments stay big, even when rolls are constant. |
| R14 | *Superseded by X1.* The look is now a bright, clean cel-shaded cartoon, matching the clean mobile-RPG style Kevin referenced (Capybara Go!), with our own characters and props. It replaces the painterly placeholder set. | Kevin's request. |
| R15 | Battle choreography: the raccoon closes to melee range, winds up and swings. Hit effects (slash trail, impact star, numbers, knockback, hit-stop on crits) play at the swing's impact frame, about 0.1 s after core resolves the hit, and an enemy stays on screen until its killing blow lands. HP bars with numbers sit under every fighter; FIGHT!, BOSS! and VICTORY! banners sweep across. | "Not just the character bumping into the other one." |
| R16 | Screen layout: battle (with the top HUD over the sky), a stat strip (Power, HP, ATK, DEF, Luck), then five tabs: Roll, Bag, Upgrades, Rebirth, Worlds. (The Shop placeholder tab gave way to Worlds.) | |

## Balance

The simulator (`npm run sim`) plays the real core with a bot that lets the reels roll, equips any bag item that beats what it wears (a simulated drag), lets the battle push through worlds on its own, taps Next 1.5 seconds after a world boss falls, buys the cheapest open hex it can afford every second (Extra Reels included), upgrades the dumpster when it can, rebirths as soon as it is allowed, and every 10 seconds jumps to the hardest world that reads Easy. With the slower climb (S1), the boss choice (S5), luck tokens (T5) and rebirth unlocks (T8), across 3 seeds over 2 hours:

- *Since R4 (Nov 4), 3 seeds over 4 hours:* World 2 at 4 to 5 minutes, World 3 at 9 to 13, World 4 at 18 to 26, World 5 and the first rebirth at 27 to 31, the second rebirth at 54 to 64, the third at 80 to 101, the fourth at 121 to 142; later runs take 40 to 50 minutes each; best world after 4 hours 15 or 16. The figures below are from before R4.
- World 2 beaten at 6.5 to 8.5 minutes, World 3 at 16 to 22, World 4 at 26 to 36, World 5 at 35 to 56, World 6 at 78 to 98 and World 7 at 85 to 106. Each world takes longer than the last.
- The first rebirth comes at 35 to 56 minutes and the second at 85 to 106; the best world after 2 hours is 7.
- Per 2 hours: about 3,500 items rolled, 18 to 22 Legendaries, 1 to 3 Mythics and now and then a Vintage. Tokens added about 20% luck on average without moving the pace (before them: first rebirth at 30 to 56 minutes, second at 84 to 111).
- Unlocks don't change power (power is rarity), only which items turn up.

Earlier results (the tap-to-dive version, the single-neighborhood roller, the nonstop roller, the small-level upgrade tree and the single reel) are in the git history.
