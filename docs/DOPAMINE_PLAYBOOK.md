# Dumpster Legend: Dopamine Playbook

Nov 2, 2026 · Kevin

> Saved verbatim from Kevin's design note. This is the feel/juice reference for every milestone,
> especially M5 (First Session and Feel). See "Status in the build" at the end for what is in so far.

Dopamine spikes hardest on anticipation and surprise, not on the prize itself. So the build-up, the unexpected, and the senses firing together matter more than what the player actually gets.

## The rules behind every hit

- **Anticipation beats payoff.** The reward system fires most when an outcome is uncertain and about to resolve. Stretch the moment before every reveal.
- **Surprise beats size.** An unexpected small win often lands harder than an expected big one. Randomize when bonuses appear.
- **Contrast makes peaks.** Quiet before loud, dark before bright, slow before fast.
- **Escalate with value.** Celebration size scales with rarity, so players read the signal before they read the item.
- **Stack the senses.** Sight, sound and touch should hit in the same frame.
- **Agency multiplies it.** When the player's own swipe, scrub or tap triggers the reveal, the win feels earned.
- **Variety prevents numbness.** The same effect repeated too often stops landing. Rotate variants.
- **Instant feedback.** Every tap gets a visible, audible response within 100 ms.

## Anticipation: the build-up

The 0.5 to 2 seconds before a reveal is where the hit happens. Every reveal needs a build-up that scales with what might be coming.

- **Rising rattle.** The lid shakes harder on each beat: 3 beats for common pulls, up to 6 for Legendary.
- **Color ladder.** The glow climbs gray, green, blue, purple, gold, pausing a little longer at each step.
- **Light leaks.** Beams escape through cracks in the lid. More beams means a rarer item.
- **Endless riser.** A pitch that seems to climb forever (a Shepard tone) plays under the rattle.
- **Heartbeat.** A low thump speeds up, matched by haptic pulses.
- **Silence drop.** Cut all audio for 0.2 seconds right before a big reveal.
- **Tunnel vision.** The screen edges darken, the background blurs, and the camera pushes in 5%.
- **Time stretch.** The last half second slows by 20%.
- **Finger peel.** Players drag the lid open slowly, like peeling a trading card. A fast swipe opens it instantly; a slow drag lets them savor it.
- **Hold to charge.** Holding the dumpster button fills a meter with a rising sound; release to dive. It is cosmetic but feels like skill.
- **Omens.** A buzzing fly hints at junk, a sparkle hints at something good, a golden glow under the bin hints at rare. Players learn to read signs.
- **Staged reveal.** Show rarity first, then the slot, then the item, then substats rolling in one at a time.
- **Substat reels.** Each substat spins like a slot reel and clicks into place, left to right.
- **Wrapped loot.** Some items come out in a knotted trash bag; tap to rip it open.
- **Dirty Legendaries.** Rare items come out filthy. The player scrubs with a finger to reveal the shine underneath, with an ASMR scrub sound.
- **Hot dumpster.** The bin smokes and glows as the bad-luck protection counter gets close, so players feel luck building. Tie it to the real counter.
- **Pity countdown.** A bar fills toward the guaranteed pull and glows hotter as it nears full.
- **Timer finale.** The last 10 seconds of any upgrade timer tick down audibly while the dumpster trembles.

## The reveal: make wins explode

The reveal hits sight, sound and touch in the same frame, and its intensity jumps at every rarity tier.

| Rarity | Visual | Sound | Haptic | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Junk | Quick pop, gray puff, stink lines | Soft rustle | None | 0.2 s |
| Decent | Green sparkle | Light chime | Single tick | 0.3 s |
| Shiny | Blue burst, shine sweep across the icon | Two-note chime | Light tap | 0.5 s |
| Epic | Purple explosion, screen tint, small shake | Rising arpeggio plus bass hit | Medium double tap | 1 s |
| Legendary | Freeze frame, white flash, gold rays, coin fountain, slow motion, name banner | Choir plus orchestral hit plus coin cascade | Heavy thump, then sparkle taps | 2.5 s |
| Mythic | All of Legendary plus a rainbow screen border, raccoon dance, Crew cheering | Unique theme sting plus crowd cheer | Rumble crescendo | 3.5 s |
| Mint in Box | Unboxing cutscene: tape slices, flaps open, spotlight drops | Announcer plus full fanfare | Custom pattern in rhythm with the fanfare | 5 s, skippable |

- **Hit-stop.** Freeze the frame for 50 to 120 ms on impact, then explode.
- **Camera punch.** A quick 3% zoom-in that springs back.
- **Flash frame.** One or two frames of white, never longer.
- **God rays.** Light rays rotate slowly behind the item.
- **Holo foil.** Epic and up get a holographic shader that shifts as the phone tilts.
- **Permanent glint.** A diagonal shine slides across rare icons every few seconds, forever. Owned loot keeps feeling special.
- **Name slam.** The item name stamps on letter by letter, each with an impact sound.
- **Overshoot landing.** The item flies into its slot, overshoots, and bounces back.
- **Power odometer.** The power score rolls up digit by digit, ticking faster as it climbs, with a pulsing green arrow.
- **Stat popups.** "+1,240 ATK" floats up in big green text.
- **Physics coins.** Coins bounce, spin and settle, then get vacuumed to the counter one by one.
- **Trash confetti.** Confetti made of banana peels, bottle caps and pizza crusts.
- **Rainbow text.** Mythic names cycle through colors.
- **Edge glow.** The screen edges glow in the rarity color and fade over 1 second.
- **NEW! stamp.** First-time Codex finds get a bouncing badge.
- **First-ever ceremony.** A player's first item of each rarity gets a one-time special stamp.
- **Watch again.** A replay button on Legendary and up, because players rewatch their best pulls.
- **Flex card.** Auto-generate a shareable image of every Legendary-plus pull with one-tap sharing.

## Sound: the reward circuit's favorite input

Sound carries much of the reward feeling, so every positive event needs a sound that still feels good on the thousandth play.

- **Pitch stacking.** Each pickup in a chain plays one semitone higher, then resets after a pause. The climbing scale itself feels like winning.
- **Same key as the music.** Tune every reward sound to the background track's key, so wins feel like the song resolving.
- **Beat sync.** Hold celebration stings until the music's next beat so they land on the downbeat.
- **Music duck and swell.** Drop the music 60% during a reveal, then swell it back up louder.
- **Signature jingle.** One short 3-note motif plays on every good thing. Players get conditioned to it, and bigger wins play longer versions.
- **Seven rarity stings.** Each tier gets its own sting, each one step more epic than the last.
- **Material ASMR.** Crinkly plastic, clanky cans, clinking glass, squishy food, each with 3 to 5 variants so nothing repeats exactly.
- **Coin cascade.** Rapid layered tinks as coins hit the counter, finished with one big ka-ching.
- **Announcer.** A hype voice yells lines like "TRASH-TASTIC!", "LEGENDARY!" and "DUMPSTER KING!", fighting-game style.
- **Raccoon voice.** Squeaks, chitters and a gleeful cackle on big pulls.
- **Crowd.** Crew members gasp, cheer and clap on big moments.
- **Sub-bass drop.** A low hit on Legendary that players feel in headphones.
- **Stereo travel.** Coins pan left to right as they fly to the counter; with headphones they swirl around the head.
- **Button pops.** Every button makes a satisfying pop with slight random pitch (plus or minus 5%) so it never grates.
- **Layered streak music.** As a streak grows, add drums, then bass, then melody. The song grows with the streak.
- **Completion dings.** A bright ding when timers finish, a shimmer when something becomes affordable.
- **Milestone fanfares.** Every 10th player level gets a bigger fanfare than normal level-ups.
- **Absurd overkill.** The rarest event plays something wildly over the top, like an opera choir over a slow-motion raccoon. Excess is the joke and the thrill.

## Haptics: wins you can feel

Vibration turns a win into something physical, and custom patterns make each rarity feel different in the hand.

- **Rarity signatures.** Decent: one tick. Shiny: two ticks. Epic: three rising taps. Legendary: heartbeat, heavy thump, then sparkle taps.
- **Anticipation heartbeat.** Pulses that speed up with the lid rattle.
- **Material textures.** Rapid micro-taps for crunchy glass, a soft long buzz for squishy food.
- **Frame-perfect impact.** The haptic fires on the exact frame of the hit-stop.
- **Streak crescendo.** Haptic strength climbs as a dive streak grows.
- **Coin taps.** Tiny taps as coins land on the counter, rate-capped so it stays crisp.
- **Boss kill rumble.** A long rolling rumble when a boss falls.
- **UI detents.** A light tick on every button and a notch-like tick when scrolling through gear.
- **Charge buzz.** Holding to charge a dive builds a buzz that intensifies until release.
- **Fanfare rhythm.** On Mint in Box, the phone vibrates in time with the music.
- **Build note.** Custom patterns need native APIs (iOS Core Haptics, Android VibrationEffect). The basic Capacitor haptics plugin only offers presets, so plan a small native plugin.
- **Player control.** On/off toggle plus an intensity slider in settings.

## Always-on juice: every tap feels good

Players spend most of their time between big wins, so the ordinary moments need small, constant hits too.

- **Squash and stretch.** Every button squishes 8% on press and bounces back.
- **Elastic motion.** Nothing in the UI moves linearly; everything eases, overshoots and settles.
- **Damage numbers.** They pop, float and fade. Crits are bigger, shakier and yellow; huge hits get an outline and linger.
- **Piñata enemies.** Enemies burst into loot when they die.
- **Overkill launches.** Massive hits send enemies flying into the sky, where they twinkle like a star.
- **Loot magnet.** Drops arc toward the raccoon and get sucked in.
- **Collect-all sweep.** One tap vacuums everything with a chain of whooshes.
- **Liquid bars.** Progress bars fill like liquid, with a shine highlight and a small overshoot at the end.
- **Breathing buttons.** Upgrade buttons glow and gently pulse when the player can afford them.
- **Claim glints.** A shine sweeps across anything ready to collect every 3 seconds.
- **Big numbers.** "+12,500 coins" feels better than "+12.5". Scale displayed values up early.
- **Multiplier tags.** "x2" and "x5" badges fly onto rewards before they land.
- **Count-ups everywhere.** Numbers never jump; they roll.
- **Living idle.** The raccoon admires its gear, polishes its weapon and sniffs the air. Neon signs flicker and rats scurry in the background.
- **Pokeable scenery.** Background trash cans wobble when tapped and sometimes drop a coin.
- **Auto-sort cascade.** The inventory sorts itself with items snapping into place one after another, each with a click.
- **Level-up shockwave.** A ring blasts out from the raccoon and knocks enemies back.
- **Flick to dismiss.** Popups fly off screen when flicked away.
- **Pulsing badges.** Notification dots pulse on anything new, used sparingly so they keep meaning something.

## Reward psychology and schedules

Unpredictable rewards on a dense schedule hold attention far longer than fixed ones, as long as a safety net keeps players from feeling cheated.

- **Variable rewards.** Most rewards should be random in timing and size. Unpredictable payouts are the stickiest schedule in behavioral psychology.
- **Fixed floors.** Daily logins, pity guarantees and bad-luck protection give a safety net under the randomness.
- **Reward density.** Something positive every 3 to 5 seconds in the alley, and a level-up every 1 to 2 minutes in the first session.
- **Near-misses.** A glow that flashes gold and lands on purple makes players want one more try. Free dives only (see the last section).
- **Head start.** New progress bars begin partly filled ("2 of 10 already done"), so players feel momentum from the start.
- **Almost there.** Players speed up near a goal, so highlight "1 more!" moments loudly.
- **Collection gaps.** "3 of 4 Bottle Caps" nags until it is finished. Show the empty slot as a silhouette.
- **Pick 1 of 3.** Letting players choose a reward increases their sense of control and investment.
- **Surprise gifts.** Unannounced drops ("A pigeon dropped something for you!") 1 to 3 times per session.
- **Lucky Hours.** Random 15-minute windows with boosted drop rates, announced with a fanfare.
- **Boxes in boxes.** Chests that contain keys to bigger chests chain several reveals together.
- **Personal records.** "New record!" pops for deepest Heist, biggest single hit and longest dive streak.
- **Power milestones.** Crossing 10K, 100K and 1M power triggers a mini ceremony.
- **Login streak flame.** Rewards escalate daily, and day 7 is a jackpot.
- **Visible pity.** "Legendary guaranteed in 12 pulls" counting down turns the grind into anticipation.
- **Overflow warning.** "Your bin is full!" nudges players back before rewards are wasted. Use it sparingly.
- **Welcome-back pile.** The offline screen shows a towering heap of trash bags that topples satisfyingly when collected.
- **Real social proof (later).** Once there is a backend, a ticker of real players' big pulls. Never invent them.

## Bonus modes, streaks, multipliers and jackpots

Short bursts where everything gets better create the peaks players remember and chase.

- **Trash Frenzy.** Every dive fills a Frenzy meter. When full: 15 seconds of free double-speed dives, boosted rarity, a rainbow border and a music change.
- **Dive streaks.** Consecutive non-junk dives build a counter that grows in size and color, with flames at 5, 10 and 20.
- **Rainbow combo.** Pulling Shiny, Epic and Legendary in one session triggers a combo bonus.
- **Double Dive.** 5% of dives randomly yield two items, with a special split-screen flash.
- **Golden Dumpster spawn.** A rare glowing bin appears with its own sound and leaves after 10 seconds. Tap it before it goes.
- **Progressive jackpot.** A visible Golden Dumpster pool grows with every dive and pays out on a rare roll. Free currency only.
- **Boss multiplier wheel.** After a boss kill, spin for x1 to x5 on the rewards, never risking what was already won.
- **Plinko drop.** Bonus coins fall through pegs into multiplier slots, with clacks and a slow final bounce.
- **Daily scratch card.** Scratching with a finger reveals symbols, with a satisfying scrape sound.
- **Chain reactions.** A Legendary pull pops nearby trash bags for free extra dives.
- **Trash Rat King.** A rare rat sprints across the alley carrying loot. Tap it before it escapes for a jackpot.
- **Boss piñata.** Bosses explode into a shower of bags; each tap pops more.
- **Double-up days.** Garbage Day Tuesdays (2x dives) and Pizza Party weekends.
- **Overdrive glow.** During Sugar Rush the raccoon glows and attacks with afterimages.

## Character, comedy and power fantasy

When the characters react to a win, the player feels it twice: once directly and once through the raccoon.

- **Raccoon emotes.** A happy dance for Epic, a jaw drop for Legendary, fainting for Mint in Box, sulking during a junk streak.
- **Mirror moment.** After equipping a Legendary, the raccoon checks itself in a cracked mirror and flexes.
- **Crew reactions.** Pigeons flap and coo, the possum plays dead from shock, Cousin Rocco goes for a high five.
- **Enemy fear.** When the raccoon outclasses a stage, enemies tremble, flee or wave white napkins in surrender.
- **Overkill comedy.** Absurd hits launch enemies off screen, followed by a distant clang as they hit a billboard.
- **Revenge replay.** Beating a boss that beat you before triggers a "Remember me?" taunt and a slow-motion final blow.
- **Silhouette upgrades.** High rarities change the raccoon's whole shape: glowing eyes, trash-bag capes, bottle-cap crowns.
- **Joke rewards.** Every item has flavor text, and rarer items get funnier, longer jokes as a reward for reading.
- **Title ceremonies.** Banners from "Alley Rookie" up to "Dumpster Legend" unlock at milestones.
- **Welcome-back scene.** Returning players find the raccoon asleep on a pile of loot; it wakes up thrilled to see them.

## Out-of-the-box: use the whole phone

The phone's sensors, screen and operating system can deliver hits that most games never touch.

- **Tilt to shimmer.** Holographic gear shifts its shine as the player tilts the phone, like a real foil card.
- **Shake the bin.** Physically shaking the phone rattles the dumpster for a dive, with matching haptics. Optional.
- **Slot lever swipe.** Swipe down on the dumpster like pulling a slot machine lever.
- **Scrub to clean.** Dirty Legendaries brighten as the player scrubs them with a finger.
- **Blow off the dust.** Blow into the microphone to dust off a Mint in Box item. Opt-in, with a permission prompt.
- **Brighter than white.** On phones with HDR screens, render the Legendary flash above normal white so it outshines everything. Native builds only.
- **Flashlight strobe.** An opt-in party mode that blinks the camera flash on Mint in Box pulls.
- **Home screen widget.** Shows the raccoon and a bin filling up, glowing when it is full.
- **Live countdowns.** iOS Live Activities and Dynamic Island, or an ongoing Android notification, for upgrade timers.
- **Golden app icon.** Unlock alternate app icons at milestones. The player's home screen becomes a trophy shelf.
- **Teaser notifications.** "Something shiny is stuck in the dumpster..." Cap at 1 to 2 per day.
- **Midnight Dive.** The in-game night follows local time, with a bonus dive window at midnight.
- **Local weather.** Rainy days bring a "Soggy Loot" bonus. Optional, needs location permission.
- **Birthday dumpster.** A player-entered birthday spawns a cake-themed bin with a guaranteed good pull.
- **One-tap flex.** Share a raccoon outfit card straight to TikTok or Instagram, framed in the rarity color.

## Session pacing: when rewards land

Front-load wins, keep something positive happening every few seconds, and end every session on a high. People remember the peak and the ending of an experience most.

| Moment | What should land |
|---|---|
| First 10 seconds ever | A guaranteed big win: the scripted Legendary |
| First 5 minutes ever | A level-up every 30 to 60 seconds and a new item every few seconds |
| Every 3 to 5 seconds while active | Something positive: a kill, a drop, a number going up |
| Every 10 to 15 minutes | One real moment: a boss kill, an Epic-plus pull or a Frenzy |
| Each return to the app | The welcome-back loot pile, tapped to burst open |
| Mid-session | One Heist or bonus mode as the session's peak |
| Before leaving | A cliffhanger: a timer almost done, pity nearly full, or a set one item from complete |
| Last thing on screen | A final reward, such as a free "goodnight dive" after finishing the daily tasks |

## Tuning rules: keep it from going numb

Effects lose their punch when overused, so ration the big ones and keep the small ones fast.

- **Match value.** Junk never gets a Legendary-sized celebration.
- **Ration peaks.** Aim for 1 to 3 "wow" moments per session. If Legendary starts to feel common, raise its rarity or its celebration.
- **Variant pools.** Make 3 to 5 versions of every sound and animation and rotate them randomly.
- **Fast repeats.** Common reveals finish in under 0.3 seconds; auto mode compresses everything.
- **Skippable.** After the first viewing, a tap skips straight to the result.
- **Snappy input.** Every tap responds within 100 ms.
- **Mute test.** Many people play with sound off, so visuals and haptics alone must still make the reveal feel great.
- **Settings.** Toggles for sound, music, haptics, reduced motion and reduced flashing.
- **Measure it.** Track session length, dives per session and day-1 retention before and after each juice change.

## Lines not to cross

A few tricks raise spending for a week, then bring refunds, store rejections, regulators and one-star reviews. Keep these out to protect the revenue.

- **Real odds only.** Displayed odds must match the code. Apple requires apps selling loot boxes or other random items to show the odds of each item type before purchase.
- **Near-misses stay free.** Use teases and "almost" effects on free dives, never on paid pulls.
- **No fake players.** Never show invented players winning. Social proof waits for real data.
- **No double-or-nothing with real money.** Risking purchased currency reads as gambling, and loot boxes already face gambling scrutiny.
- **Clear purchase buttons.** Every purchase needs an unmistakable confirmation. The FTC made Epic Games pay $245 million in refunds after confusing Fortnite buttons triggered unwanted charges with a single press.
- **Protect kids.** A cartoon raccoon will attract children. Add an age gate and require parental consent for purchases by younger players; the same FTC case cited kids buying with no parental involvement.
- **Flash safety.** Never flash more than 3 times per second, and offer a reduce-flashing setting.
- **Spending tools.** Show purchase history and offer optional spending limits. Big spenders who feel tricked churn and file chargebacks.

## Status in the build

What the build already does from this playbook (the rest is scheduled in `DECISIONS.md`):

- Scripted Legendary on the very first roll (first few seconds).
- Constant small wins: the dumpster's prize wheel spins on its own and lands an item every 2.6 seconds, faster with the Spin Speed upgrade and Trash Bag turbo spins.
- A visible variable-ratio beat: the Lucky Spin meter fills toward a x2-luck spin, and once unlocked, a Golden Spin (x5 luck) can strike any spin.
- Legendary and better: while the wheel spins, it glows in the rarity's color with rising rattle beats, a riser and a silence drop; when it lands, a full-screen reveal (rays, confetti, giant "1 in X" odds, NEW BEST FIND!). Big rolls never get lost; they queue.
- Every item wears its "1 in X" odds on a big banner (pulsing for Epic, gold for Legendary, rainbow for Mythic), so the bag itself shows how lucky you got.
- Equipping is hands-on: you drag the item onto the raccoon, and it puts it on right there in the fight.
- The upgrade map only shows one step past what you can buy, as "?" hexes, so every purchase reveals something new; affordable hexes glow with a red "!".
- Reveal intensity scales by tier (pop → sting + burst for Epic → full reveal for Legendary, Mythic and Mint).
- Haptics per tier using Capacitor presets (custom patterns need the native plugin in M5).
- Bad-luck protection with a visible "Epic+ guaranteed in N" readout.
- Every hit lands with a slash, an impact star and a number; crits add hit-stop, a bigger number and a shake; coins arc up into the gold counter.
- Squash on every button press; instant (< 100 ms) visual response on tap; count-up numbers.
- Flash safety: at most one flash per reveal; Reduce Flashing removes it, Reduce Motion removes shakes and lunges.
- Breathing buttons when a boss fight or a Dumpster upgrade is ready, and "!" badges on tabs with something to do.
- Odds screen generated from the same function the code rolls with; the odds shown are the real ones.
